# TACL review formatting

The manuscript follows the [official TACL formatting instructions](https://transacl.org/tacl-submission-templates/tacl2021v1-submission-formatting-instructions.pdf), with the appendix length/content rules superseded by the [March 2024 appendix policy](https://transacl.org/index.php/tacl/announcement/view/105).

Use A4, two columns, 11-point main text, captions at least 10 points, embedded fonts, anonymous authorship, page numbers, marginal review rulers, and the confidentiality header. Use the unmodified official TACL style in its default submission mode. Keep table and figure contents legible; do not reduce body or caption sizes to fit.

Initial submissions have seven to ten main-content pages, excluding references. After references, allow at most five pages of replication details and three pages of complementary tables/figures explicitly cited in the main paper. Appendices are unreviewed and may not carry free analytical prose except replication details. Keep both categories in the submitted PDF and count them separately.

Rebuild and visually inspect the PDF after edits; inspect author metadata, all page boundaries, captions, figures, table widths, citations, and reference resolution. See README.md for the manuscript's current page allocation and outstanding author checks. The legacy ACL conference templates and bibliography test fixtures in this directory do not define TACL submission requirements.
